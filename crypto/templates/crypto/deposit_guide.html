How to Make a Deposit

To complete your first deposit, please follow these steps:

  1. Sign up and create your investor account.
  2. Upload your wallet address securely to your profile.
  3. Navigate to the Make Deposit option in your dashboard.
  4. Select your preferred payment method (USDT, BTC, Ethereum, etc.).
  5. Confirm the transaction to finalize your deposit.
