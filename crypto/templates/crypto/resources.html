Crypto Glossary

A comprehensive glossary of essential crypto and blockchain terms, designed to help investors, traders, and enthusiasts understand the language of digital finance.

  • Blockchain: A decentralized digital ledger that records transactions across multiple computers securely.
  • Bitcoin (BTC): The first and most widely recognized cryptocurrency, often referred to as digital gold.
  • Ethereum (ETH): A blockchain platform enabling smart contracts and decentralized applications (dApps).
  • Altcoin: Any cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin, such as Litecoin, Cardano, or Solana.
  • DeFi: Decentralized Finance — financial services built on blockchain without traditional intermediaries.
  • Wallet: A digital tool (software or hardware) used to store and manage cryptocurrencies securely.
  • Mining: The process of validating transactions and securing a blockchain network, often rewarded with new coins.
  • Stablecoin: A cryptocurrency pegged to a stable asset (like USD) to reduce volatility.
