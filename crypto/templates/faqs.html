Everything you need to know about trading and investing on BitnetFX

Account & Registration

How do I create an account?

Click “Register” at the top right, fill in your details, and verify your email.

Is registration free?

Yes, creating an account on BitnetFX is completely free.

Can I open multiple accounts?

No, each user is allowed only one account to ensure compliance and security.

Deposits & Withdrawals

What payment methods are supported?

We support Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, and major fiat gateways depending on your region.

How long do withdrawals take?

Crypto withdrawals are processed instantly once approved. Fiat withdrawals may take 1–3 business days.

Is there a minimum deposit?

Yes, the minimum deposit is $50 or equivalent in crypto.

Trading

What instruments can I trade?

BitnetFX offers cryptocurrencies, forex pairs, commodities, and indices.

Do you offer demo accounts?

Yes, you can practice trading with a demo account before switching to live trading.

Are there trading fees?

We charge competitive spreads and a small commission depending on the instrument.

Security

How secure is BitnetFX?

We use SSL encryption, two‑factor authentication, and cold storage for crypto assets.

What is 2FA?

Two‑Factor Authentication adds an extra layer of security by requiring a code from your mobile device when logging in.

Support

How can I contact support?

Email us at support@bitnetfx.com or use live chat available 24/7.

Do you provide educational resources?

Yes, we offer tutorials, webinars, and trading guides to help you improve your skills.

Compliance & Regulations

Is BitnetFX regulated?

Yes, we comply with international KYC/AML standards and operate under financial regulations in our jurisdictions.

Do I need to verify my identity?

Yes, identity verification is required to comply with anti‑money laundering laws and ensure account security.
